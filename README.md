# 🛒 D-Mart Business Intelligence Dashboard
An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze D-Mart's retail performance, customer behavior, product trends, and business KPIs.

## 📊 Dashboard Preview

## 🎯 Project Objective
The objective of this project is to transform retail transaction data
into meaningful business insights and support data-driven decision making.

## 📌 Key KPIs (Page-1: Sales Overview)
- Total Revenue
- Unique Customers
- Total Orders
- Average Order Value
- Delivery Rate
- Cancellation Rate

## 📌 Key KPIs (Page-2: Sales Insights)
- Average Discount
- Average Rating
- Average Shipping Charge
- Return Rate
- Revenue per Customer
- 
## 📈 Key Analysis (Page 1)
- Top 3 products
- Revenue over State
- Revenue over State
- Orders over payment method

## 📈 Key Analysis (Page 2)
- Revenue vs Marketing
- Revenue vs Subscription
- Total Revenue by Ship mode
  
## 💡 Dynamic Business Insights
Which is the most efficient Marketing Channel with less returns and more revenue.
